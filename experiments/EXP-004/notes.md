# EXP-004 spatial convergence audit

Uniform backward-Euler time step is fixed at `dt=0.0625 s`. Only `dr` changes: 0.1, 0.05, 0.025 cm; `dr=0.0125 cm` (N=160) is the independent reference. Full-time CSVs contain signed and absolute errors at surface, r=1.5 cm, r=1.0 cm, and center, with L∞ and discrete RMS-L2 over 0--1800 s.
